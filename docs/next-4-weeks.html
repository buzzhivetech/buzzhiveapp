What not to do for the next four weeks

  • No engineering sprints.
    The entire point of Phase 0 is to decide whether to build, not to build while deciding. The temptation to "just polish the dashboard" or "add one more sensor feature" is the single biggest risk to the sprint.
  • No full fundraise conversations.
    Exploratory investor calls for calibration are fine. Pitching an actual raise is not. Without Phase 0 evidence, you'll anchor low on valuation and lose leverage before you have the story to support it.
  • No broad announcement of the strategic reframe.
    Keep positioning quiet until the memo is written. Loose talk before validation creates brand confusion that's expensive to unwind if Phase 0 kills the thesis.
  • No extending Phase 0 "for one more call."
    Ship the synthesis on time. Scope creep is the mode of failure that looks most like progress. If the signal is unclear after 12 calls, two more calls won't fix it.
  • No pitching instead of diagnosing on Phase 0 calls.
    The moment you start describing a feature set, you're in sales mode. Contacts will be polite, data will be noise. If you hear yourself explaining the product for more than 90 seconds, stop and ask a question.

Two things most likely to go wrong

Risk 01 · Insurance axis ghosts
MGAs don't respond to cold outreach at high rates.
Response rates for cold MGA outreach run 20–30%. If by end of week 2 you're at zero calls booked in the insurance axis, escalate immediately. Ask every board member for one warm intro. Consider a short LinkedIn post surfacing whether anyone in the extended network knows someone at Arbol or Floodbase. The insurance signal is too important to let slip through inaction.
Risk 02 · Phase 0 drifts into sales
When conversations go well, diagnostic discipline breaks down.
The most common Phase 0 failure mode is not poor response rates — it's slipping into pitch mode because the calls are warm. Re-read the anti-patterns section of the phase-0-playbook at the end of week 2 and week 3. Hold the line. A polite yes without the kill question is worthless data.
