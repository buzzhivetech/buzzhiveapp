02
Beachhead → platform

Two horizons, one deployment

Same sensors  ·  Same ground truth
Horizon 1 — Years 1–2

Sideliner winter-survival SaaS

A hardware + software product for stationary beekeepers managing 15–100 hives. Answers the question nobody else does: which of my hives won't make spring?

  • $99 sensor, $60/hive/year software
  • Weight + internal climate + bee activity
  • Multi-apiary triage dashboard
  • Winter-survival score (our first real AI)
  • NE + Upper Midwest launch
Horizon 2 — Years 3–5

Honey bee data platform

Every deployed sensor becomes a labeled datapoint. Aggregate insights become a defensible asset nobody else owns. Revenue stacks on top of the SaaS base.

  • Data licensing to research + universities
  • AI API for other sector platforms
  • CPG / ESG sustainability reporting
  • Crop-insurance loss modeling
  • Natural acquisition target for BeeHero-tier

04
Operating pillars

Four things we refuse to compromise on

Everything else is negotiable

Reliability

Hardware that doesn't churn

Multi-year battery. Weatherproof. No false positives. Every competitor's graveyard is unreliable hardware. We don't ship until our pilots keep sensors in the hive through a full winter.

Decisions

Instructions, not dashboards

Every alert ends with a verb. "Add a winter patty within 7 days." Not a squiggly line. We sell decisions, not data.

Labeled data

Every hive deposits a moat

Sensor + inspection outcome = labeled training data. Every year's winter is a dataset no competitor has. The moat compounds.

Customer-led

Ship from the survey outward

25 yes-responses + 8 maybes are in hand. Every product decision gets validated against real pilots before we build it. No roadmap theater.

North star

Every hive we deploy is a customer, a labeled datapoint, and a node in the network that makes the platform real.
